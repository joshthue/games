How to play

On your turn, roll all six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die, then choose: roll the dice that are left to build your turn higher, or bank what you've got.

If a roll shows no scoring dice at all, that's a Farkle — your whole unbanked turn is lost. Bank before your luck runs out.

Clear all six dice and you've got hot dice: roll all six again and keep stacking.
