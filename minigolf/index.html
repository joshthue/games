How to play

One big championship green with nine pins cut across it. Tap a flag (or its number below) to pick the pin you’re putting to. Pull back from the ball to aim and set pace — further back is harder — then let go.

Reading the green

  • Contours (the shaded tiers and hills) push the ball as it rolls. A putt across a slope breaks downhill.
  • Grain is the way the grass lies, shown by the fine streaks. Downgrain runs the ball out; into the grain it dies short; across it the putt breaks. On a real green the grain is half the read.
  • Run off the putting surface into the fringe and the ball dies fast — leave yourself on the green.

Scoring

Count your putts on each pin. Hole all nine, then see your total — a 2-putt average is solid; go lower and you’re a real putter. Cut new pins re-cuts all nine for a fresh round. Replay pin re-drops the current one.
