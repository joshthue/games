President

Shed your whole hand first to become President; finish last and you're the Scum. Play singles, pairs or triples, each beaten only by the same number of higher cards (2 is highest). Each new round, the Scum hands their best cards up to the President. First to the points target wins.

How to play

Everyone gets 13 cards. The lead plays any number of cards of one rank (a single, pair, or triple). Each player in turn must play the same number of cards at a higher rank, or pass.

Ranks run 3 (low) up through king, ace, and 2 is the highest. Once you pass you're out until the pile clears. When everyone else passes, the last player to lay cards clears the pile and leads again.

First to empty their hand is President, then Vice-President, then Vice-Scum; last is the Scum. Next round the Scum must give the President their two best cards (the President returns two unwanted ones), and the Vice-Scum and VP swap one.

Scoring each round: President 3, VP 2, Vice-Scum 1, Scum 0. First to the target wins.
