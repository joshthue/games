How Horse Race works

Take the Aces and Kings out of a deck. The 44 cards left are the horses: 2 through 10, Jack is 11, Queen is 12. They're all dealt out, so each card you hold is a share in that horse. Four 7s means you own the whole 7.

1 · Scratches

The dice come around the table. The first four different totals rolled are scratched: the 1st costs $1 a card, the 2nd $2, the 3rd $3, the 4th $4. Everybody pays that for each card of it they hold, into the pot. A total that's already scratched just rolls again.

2 · The race

Every roll moves that horse one step. Roll a scratched number and you pay its scratch price into the pot. The first horse to the finish wins, and the pot is split four ways — one share per card of the winning number. Hold two of them, get half.

Live

One track, everybody who has Horse Race open — no rooms, no codes. Get in the next race, and anyone at the rail can call the horses to the gate. Bots fill the rest of the table — 2 to 8 seats, your pick under Table & names, where you can also rename yourself, the bots and the horses. Take your turn tapping Roll; if somebody's phone is in their pocket, the table rolls for them after a few seconds. Money comes out of the same wallet as Pull Tabs.
