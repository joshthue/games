How to play

Everybody antes into the pot. Then the deals go 3 cards, 5 cards, 7 cards, and back to 3, around and around until somebody has enough legs.

After each deal, everyone decides in or out at the same time — at the table it's a chip in a closed fist, "1, 2, 3, drop." Here you tap In or Out, and the bots have already made up their minds.

  • Two or more in: best hand wins a leg. Every other player who stayed in matches the pot (up to the match cap), so the pot grows.
  • Only one in: they win a leg free (unless "Alone in" is set to win nothing).
  • Nobody in: nothing happens; on to the next deal.
  • Tie: everyone tied for best gets a leg and nobody tied pays.

Forced (optional, in More options): each player's first card is dealt face up, and whoever shows the highest upcard — everyone tied for it — must stay in. A forced player who loses pays only half the match.

First to the legs target (4 by default) takes the whole pot, and a new game starts with a fresh ante.

Wild cards

By default each deal's own number is wild: 3s in the 3-card deal, 5s in the 5-card, 7s in the 7-card. A wild can be any card, so five of a kind is possible and beats a straight flush. Wilds are outlined in gold.

Hands

3 cards: three of a kind, then a pair, then high card (straights and flushes count only if you turn them on). 5 and 7 cards: regular poker — your best five — from five of a kind down to high card.

Your net is kept across games for the whole session; the Menu starts a new one.
