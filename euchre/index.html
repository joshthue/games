Euchre

The Midwest classic. You and North against West and East, 24 cards, five a hand. Name trump, then take three of the five tricks. First team to 10 wins.

How to play

Four players, two teams: you and North across from West and East. A 24-card deck (9 through ace), five cards each. One card is turned up on the kitty.

Naming trump

  • Round one: starting left of the dealer, each player may order up the turned suit as trump, or pass. If it’s ordered up, the dealer takes that card and discards one.
  • Round two: if everyone passes, each player may name any other suit, or pass. With stick the dealer on, the dealer must name one if it comes back around.
  • The team that names trump are the makers. A maker may go alone — their partner sits out the hand.

The bowers

The jack of trump is the right bower — the highest card. The other jack of the same colour is the left bower, second-highest, and it counts as a trump (not its printed suit).

Scoring (to 10)

  • Makers take 3 or 4 tricks: 1 point. All 5: 2. Alone and all 5: 4.
  • Makers take fewer than 3 (euchred): the other team scores 2.

Teaching mode

Outlines the cards you can legally play in green, marks your trump, suggests whether to order it up, and explains the hint.
