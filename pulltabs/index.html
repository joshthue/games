How pull tabs work

Every deal is a box of 400 tickets. The winners are already in there — a fixed number of each prize, printed on the flare (the prize sheet on the wall). Buy a ticket, pull back its five tabs, and three matching symbols across a row wins what the flare says for that row.

The flare keeps score: a red dot is a prize somebody has already pulled, a green one is still in the box. When the box is down to its last hundred and the big one is still green — that's when folks start buying.

The seal

Some tickets have ✍️✍️✍️. That one doesn't pay cash — it puts your name on the next line of the seal board. When all eight lines are signed, Darlene peels the seal and the hidden line number wins the seal prize ($50 on a $1 deal, $100 on the Uff Da 7s).

The money

It's play money, but it sticks around between visits. The ATM gives you $20 and charges a $3 fee, because it's a VFW. Net is your cash minus everything you've pulled out of the ATM — how far ahead (or behind) you really are.

Shared box

One phone starts the box and reads out the 4-letter code; everybody else joins with it and buys from the same box. The phone that started it holds the box — keep it open. When somebody hits big, it pops up on everyone's phone.

Pays back about 86% across the whole box, seal included. Proceeds benefit the Lake Uffda Lions — new dock at the public access.
