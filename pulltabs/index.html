How pull tabs work

Every deal is a box of 400 tickets. The winners are already in there — a fixed number of each prize, printed on the flare (the prize sheet on the wall).

Each ticket has three tabs over a square of nine pictures. A winner has an arrow (or on some deals a red bar) printed through three squares in a line — across, down, or corner to corner — over one of the combos on the flare: two of the deal's key picture and a prize picture, with the arrowhead on the prize. Check the flare to see what your combo pays — now and then a ticket also prints a yellow $ TOTAL box. And if you peel back the top tab and see a yellow serial-number sticker — hold onto your hat, hon, that’s one of the big ones. Multiple winners: some tickets have two lines, and the TOTAL adds them up. An arrow going down or slantwise runs under all three tabs, so you might not know till the last one.

Tap a tab to rip it open, or grab its right edge (OPEN HERE) and slide it left — it peels back from the right like the real ones, and if you let go part way it stays peeked. Not sure about one? Set it aside and pick it back up later. Toss it sends it to the bucket — Darlene checks it first, so a winner never goes in the trash.

The flare keeps score: a red dot is a prize somebody has already pulled, a green one is still in the box. When the box is down to its last hundred and the big one is still green — that's when folks start buying.

The money

It's play money, but it sticks around between visits. You walk in with $100, and tabs go by the 20 — nobody buys one tab. The ATM gives you another $100 and charges a $3 fee, because it's a VFW. Net is your cash minus everything you brought and pulled out of the ATM — how far ahead (or behind) you really are.

The booth

All six boxes sit on the counter at once. Each one shows its stack of tabs going down as people buy, and whether its top prize is still in there. Tap a box to buy from it and see its flare. A sold-out box sits there empty until somebody wants that deal again — then Darlene cracks a fresh one.

Live

There's one booth and everybody's in it — no rooms, no codes. Walk up and you're buying from the same six boxes as everyone else who has Pull Tabs open, watching them go down together in real time. Big winners scroll across the ticker up top (and pop up when they happen), and you'll hear about it when a box gets down to its last fifty with the top prize still in.

Pays back about 83% across the whole box — one ticket in eight is a winner. Proceeds benefit the Lake Uffda Lions — new dock at the public access.
