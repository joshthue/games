How pull tabs work

Every deal is a box of 400 tickets. The winners are already in there — a fixed number of each prize, printed on the flare (the prize sheet on the wall).

Each ticket has three tabs over a square of nine pictures. A winner has an arrow printed through three squares in a line — across, down, or corner to corner — and a yellow $ TOTAL box somewhere else on the ticket that says what it pays. The pictures under the arrow don't have to match. An arrow going down or slantwise runs under all three tabs, so you might not know till the last one.

Tap a tab to rip it open, or slide it: let go part way and it stays peeked. Not sure about one? Set it aside and pick it back up later. Toss it sends it to the bucket — Darlene checks it first, so a winner never goes in the trash.

The flare keeps score: a red dot is a prize somebody has already pulled, a green one is still in the box. When the box is down to its last hundred and the big one is still green — that's when folks start buying.

The money

It's play money, but it sticks around between visits. The ATM gives you $20 and charges a $3 fee, because it's a VFW. Net is your cash minus everything you've pulled out of the ATM — how far ahead (or behind) you really are.

Shared box

One phone starts the box and reads out the 4-letter code; everybody else joins with it and buys from the same box. The phone that started it holds the box — keep it open. When somebody hits big, it pops up on everyone's phone.

Pays back about 83% across the whole box — one ticket in eight is a winner. Proceeds benefit the Lake Uffda Lions — new dock at the public access.
