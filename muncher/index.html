How to play

• Eat every berry to clear the board.
• Glow-caps (the four big ones) make the wisps flee — eat them for bonus points, worth more each in a row.
• A wisp touching you costs a life. Clear the maze to move up a level; the wisps get quicker and the glow wears off sooner.
• The side tunnels wrap left↔right.
