3-5-7 — Privacy Policy

3-5-7 does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The game runs entirely on your device. Your settings, the game in progress, your bankroll and your stats are saved only on your device, so they are there next time you play. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It makes no network connections.

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.
