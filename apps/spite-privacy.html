Grandma Jo's Spite & Malice — Privacy Policy

Spite & Malice does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The game runs entirely on your device. Player names you type, your settings, and your win/loss records are saved only on your device, so they can be there next time you play. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the app signs in to it at launch so Apple's Game Center features can work. That connection is between your device and Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; we receive nothing from it. The app makes no other network connections.

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.
