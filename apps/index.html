Game Night apps — Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas for any of these apps: email joshthue@gmail.com. I read everything.

Grandma Jo's Spite & Malice

The classic race card game against a bot, offline. Privacy policy

Royal Draw

Poker solitaire: deal 25 cards into a 5×5 grid and score the ten poker hands. Levels, Blitz boards and a night theme. Privacy policy

3-5-7

The bar poker card game: deal 3, 5, then 7 cards, each deal's number wild, drop in or out, best hand still in wins a leg, first to the legs target takes the pot. Against bots, offline. Privacy policy

Common questions

Do the apps need an internet connection? No. Everything runs on your device.

Where are my records? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Both games are also free to play in a browser at joshthue.github.io/games.
