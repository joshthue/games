Reversi

Also called Othello. Trap the bot's discs between two of yours to flip them to your colour. Most discs when the board fills wins. Dark moves first.

How to play

Place a disc so that one or more of the bot's discs sit in an unbroken line between your new disc and another of yours — in any direction. Every trapped disc flips to your colour.

You must flip at least one disc, or you pass. When neither side can move, whoever has more discs wins.

Corners can never be flipped — they're the key to the board. Be wary of handing one over by playing right next to an empty corner.
