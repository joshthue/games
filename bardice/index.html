🎲 Shake of the Day

A dollar gets you three shakes of five dice. Every day has its own number (it changes at midnight). Between shakes, hold the dice you like and shake the rest. Finish with all five showing today's number and the jackpot is yours.

Every try adds a dollar to the jackpot, and everybody with Bar Dice open is shaking for the same one — the board shows who's been in today. Four of the number gets you a Hamm's on Darlene ($2 back). After a win the jackpot starts again at $25.

Odds, holding the number: about 1 try in 75 hits all five; about 1 in 11 gets four.

⚓ Ship, Captain & Crew

Everyone antes into the pot. On your turn you get three rolls of five dice. You need a 6 (ship), then a 5 (captain), then a 4 (crew) — in that order, though they can all land in the same roll. Darlene sets them aside for you as they come.

Once the crew's aboard, your other two dice are the cargo: their total is your score. If you have rolls left you can stand on it or roll the cargo again. No ship, captain and crew after three rolls and you sink with nothing. Best cargo takes the pot; a tie splits it.
