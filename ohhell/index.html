How to play

Each round everyone is dealt the same number of cards, and one more card is turned up to set the trump suit. The hand grows by one card every round.

Going round from the dealer's left, each player bids the exact number of tricks they expect to win. With screw the dealer on, the dealer can't bid the number that would make the bids total the hand size — someone has to be wrong.

Then play tricks: follow the led suit if you can; trumps beat everything else; highest card wins.

SCORING

Take exactly your bid: 10 + your bid. Take any other number: 0. Highest total after the last round wins.
