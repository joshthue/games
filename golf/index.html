How to play

The swing

Three taps, the way golf games have always done it. Tap once to start the meter moving. Tap again at the top to set your power. Tap a third time as the marker comes back through the centre line to set your accuracy — early pushes it right, late pulls it left.

You can swing past 100% into the red for extra yards, but the meter moves faster up there and a miss costs you more.

Clubs and lies

Distances shown are what the club carries from a clean lie. The rough takes yards off and kills your roll; sand takes more and you can't hit anything longer than a 7-iron out of it. Water and out of bounds cost you a stroke and you play from where you were.

On the green

The meter becomes a putting stroke and the view drops to an overhead read. Watch the slope arrow: the ball breaks as it rolls, and downhill putts run out.
