How to play

  • Bet One adds a coin (1 to 5, then back to 1). Bet Max bets five and deals.
  • Deal gives you five cards. Tap the ones you want to keep: they say HELD. Tap again to let one go.
  • Draw replaces every card you didn't hold, from the same deck. The pay table lights up what you hit and the credits roll up.

Paying

Wins are coins per coin bet: the column lit red is your bet. A royal flush on five coins pays 4,000 instead of 1,250, so real players always bet max. Lowest win is shown at the bottom of the table; anything less loses the bet.

The games

  • Jacks or Better 9/6: the classic. A pair of jacks or better gets your bet back.
  • Bonus Poker 8/5: four aces and four 2s-4s pay extra.
  • Double Double Bonus 9/6: four aces with a 2, 3 or 4 kicker pays 400 a coin. Two pair only pays even money.
  • Deuces Wild: every 2 is wild. Three of a kind is the lowest win; four deuces pays 200 a coin.

Money

Credits are your cash in the bar wallet (the same one Pull Tabs, Horse Race and Bar Dice use) counted in the coin you picked. Short? The ATM gives $100 for a $3 fee.
