How to play

Everybody plays solitaire at the same time, each with their own deck, onto piles in the middle that belong to nobody. No turns: whoever's fastest gets there first.

Your cards

  • Nerts pile (bottom left): 13 cards, only the top one showing. Get rid of these.
  • Work piles: four of them. Build down in alternating colours (red 7 on a black 8). Move a card or a run between them. An empty work pile takes any card.
  • Stock (bottom right): tap it to turn over three onto the waste. Play the waste's top card. When it runs out, tap again to start it over.

The middle

Any ace starts a pile. Then up by suit to the king, by anybody. Every card you get in there is +1.

Playing on the phone

  • Tap a card: if it fits the middle it goes there. If not, it's picked up (gold); tap a work pile to drop it.
  • Drag a card (or a run from a work pile) to a work pile or the middle.

Calling Nerts

Empty your Nerts pile and the round stops for everybody. Score +1 per card you got into the middle and −2 per card left in your Nerts pile. If everybody's stuck for a while, each stock moves its top card to the bottom; if that stops helping, the round ends where it is. First to the target wins.
