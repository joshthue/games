Dump

The shedding game — you and three bots. Match the top card by colour, number or symbol; play wilds to switch colours; and stack a Draw Two on a Draw Two to pass the pain along. First to empty their hand wins.

How to play

You and three bots, seven cards each. On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the pile by colour, number, or symbol — or a Wild to change the colour. Empty your hand first to win.

The action cards

  • Skip (⊘) — the next player misses a turn.
  • Reverse (⇆) — turn order flips.
  • Draw Two (+2) — the next player draws two and is skipped… unless they stack their own Draw Two on top, passing the total along. Whoever can’t stack takes all of it.
  • Wild — play on anything, then choose the colour.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4) — choose the colour and the next player draws four, or stacks their own +4 to pass it on.

When you’re stuck

Can’t play? Draw — you keep drawing until you turn up a card you can play, then you may play it (or pass if the pile runs out). Down to one card, the game calls “Last card!” for you automatically.
