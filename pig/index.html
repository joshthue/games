Pig

The simplest press-your-luck there is. Roll the die and keep adding it up — but roll a 1 and your turn's points vanish. Hold to bank them. First to 100 wins.

How to play

On your turn, roll the die as many times as you like. Every roll of 2–6 adds to your turn pot.

Roll a 1 and the whole pot is gone — your turn ends with nothing.

Hold any time to bank the pot into your score and pass the die along.

First player to 100 wins. The trick is knowing when enough is enough — most good players hold around 20.
