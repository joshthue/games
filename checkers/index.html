Checkers

Move your pieces up the board on the diagonals and jump the bot's. Captures are forced, doubles chain, and a piece that reaches the far row is crowned a king. Clear the board to win.

How to play

Your pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. You're at the bottom, moving up.

Jumps are mandatory. If you can leap a bot piece into an empty square just beyond it, you must — and if the same piece can keep jumping, the whole chain is one move.

Reach the far row and your piece is crowned a king — kings move and jump in any diagonal direction.

Take all the bot's pieces, or leave it with no legal move, and you win.
