How to play

Goal: be the first to empty your face-up goal pile (bottom-left). The card on top of your goal pile is your most important card — play it whenever you can.

The centre

There are four shared centre stacks. Each is built upward from Ace to King. A stack starts with an Ace (or a wild), then 2, 3, 4 … up to King. A completed A→K stack is cleared away and recycled into the stock.

Your turn

  • You start each turn with 5 cards in hand.
  • Play cards to the centre from three places: the top of your goal pile, your hand, or the top of any side stack.
  • Empty your hand by playing? You immediately draw back up to 5 and keep going.
  • End your turn by moving one hand card to a side stack (your four discard piles). You can play to the centre or discard by tapping.

Wilds

Jokers are wild — play one on any stack and it becomes whatever value that stack needs (or an Ace to start a stack). Save them for the cards you can’t place.

Strategy

  • The centre is shared — advancing a stack can help the bot’s goal card too, so watch its top goal card before you play.
  • Organise your side stacks in descending order (low cards on top) so you can dig them back out.
