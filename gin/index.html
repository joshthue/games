Gin Rummy

Draw and discard to build melds — three or four of a kind, or runs in a suit. Get your leftover "deadwood" to 10 or less and knock; clear it all for gin. First to 100 wins.

How to play

Each turn: draw one card (from the stock, or the top of the discard pile), then discard one. You're building melds:

  • a set — three or four of the same rank,
  • a run — three or more in sequence in one suit.

Cards not in a meld are deadwood (face cards 10, ace 1). When your deadwood is 10 or less you can knock: both hands are revealed, you may lay your dead cards onto the opponent's melds, and the lower deadwood wins the difference.

Zero deadwood is gin — a 25-point bonus and no lay-offs allowed. Beat or tie the knocker and you undercut them for 25 plus the difference. First to 100 wins.
