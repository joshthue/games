Chinese Checkers

Ten marbles each. Get them all into the point across the star before anybody else. Hop over any marble, as many times in a row as you can.

How to play

Everybody starts with ten marbles in one point of the star. Be first to get all of yours into the point straight across.

On your turn, one of these

  • Step one marble into a neighbouring empty hole, any direction.
  • Hop over a neighbouring marble (anybody's) into the empty hole straight beyond. Keep hopping with the same marble as long as you can, in any directions. Nothing gets captured.

A step and a hop can't go in the same move.

On the phone

  • Tap one of your marbles: every hole it can reach lights up (gold rings are the long hop chains). Tap one and it goes.
  • Hint shows the move the Normal bot would make. Undo takes back your last move and the bots' answers.

Winning

Fill every hole of your target point. If somebody leaves a marble parked there, you still win once the point is full and at least one of the marbles in it is yours.
