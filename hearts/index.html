How to play

Four players, thirteen cards each. Points are bad: every heart you take is 1, the queen of spades is 13. Lowest score wins when somebody reaches the target.

The pass

Before each hand you pass three cards: to the left, then the right, then across, then a hand with no pass, and round again.

The tricks

  • Whoever holds the 2♣ leads it to the first trick.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play anything. The highest card of the suit led takes the trick and leads the next one.
  • No hearts and no Q♠ on the first trick, unless that's all you have.
  • You can't lead a heart until one has been played (hearts are broken), unless you have nothing else.

Shooting the moon

Take all thirteen hearts and the queen: you score 0 and everybody else gets 26.

On the phone

  • Tap a card to lift it, tap it again to play it. Dimmed cards can't be played right now.
  • Passing: tap three cards, then Pass. The cards you got back are outlined in blue.
  • Hint lifts the card the Normal bot would play. Last trick shows the trick that just went.
