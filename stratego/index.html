THE ARMY

Lower number wins. Equal ranks both die. Only a Miner (8) survives a Bomb. The Spy (S) kills the Marshal only when the Spy attacks — defending, it dies to anything. Scouts (9) move any distance in a straight line and may strike at the end of the run. Bombs and the Flag never move.
