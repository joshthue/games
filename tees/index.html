How to play

The triangle has 15 holes and 14 tees. Leave as few tees standing as you can.

Start

Tap any tee to pull it out. That's your empty hole. (Every starting hole can be finished with one tee.)

Jumping

  • Tap a tee, then a glowing hole. Or drag the tee there.
  • A tee jumps over one neighbour into an empty hole, in a straight line: along a row or either slant.
  • The tee it jumps comes out.

The end

When no tee can jump, count what's left. One is genius; two is pretty sharp; three is respectable. Hint shows a jump that keeps your best finish alive; Undo takes back as many as you like.
