How to play

Everyone rolls five dice and hides them under a cup. You can see only your own.

Players take turns bidding — a quantity and a face, like "three 5s" — claiming that at least that many of that face are on the table, counting everyone's dice.

Aces (1s) are wild and count as whatever face is bid.

Each bid must be higher than the last: more dice, or the same number at a higher face.

Think the last bid is a bluff? Call Liar. Everyone lifts their cups and the bid face (plus aces) is counted:

  • If there are enough, the bid was good — you lose a die for the bad call.
  • If there aren't, the bidder was bluffing — they lose a die.

Lose all five dice and you're out. Last player with dice wins.
