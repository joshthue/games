Rummy

Draw and discard to build melds — sets or runs — and lay them on the table. Lay off spare cards onto anyone's melds. Empty your hand first to win the round; everyone else is docked the cards left in theirs. First to 100 wins.

How to play

On your turn: draw a card (stock or the top discard), then you may lay melds and lay off, and finally discard one card.

  • A set is 3-4 of the same rank; a run is 3+ in sequence in one suit.
  • Tap cards to select them, then lay the meld onto the table.
  • Select a single card and lay off to extend any meld already on the table — yours or a bot's.

Empty your hand to go out and win the round. Everyone else is charged the pip value of the cards still in their hand (face cards 10, ace 1). First to 100 points wins.
