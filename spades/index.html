How to play

Four players, two partnerships: you and North (across from you) against West and East. Thirteen cards each. Spades are always trump.

The bid

  • Everyone bids how many of the 13 tricks they expect to take. Your bid and your partner’s add up to your team’s contract.
  • Nil is a bid of 0 — a promise to take no tricks at all. Make it for +100, break it for −100.
  • Blind nil (if turned on) is a nil bid made before you look at your hand, for ±200.

The play

  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can’t, play anything — including a spade to trump.
  • The highest spade wins the trick; if no spade was played, the highest card of the led suit wins.
  • You can’t lead a spade until spades are “broken” (someone has trumped in), unless spades are all you hold.

Scoring

  • Make your contract: 10 points per bid trick. Miss it: −10 per bid trick.
  • Extra tricks are bags: +1 each, but every 10 bags costs you 100. Don’t overbid, and don’t pile up overtricks.
  • First team to the target (default 300) wins; a team at −200 loses.

Teaching mode

Turn it on and the game outlines every card you’re allowed to play in green, explains the rule when you tap one you can’t, suggests a bid, and tells you why the hint card is the play. Hint and Last trick are under the hand.
