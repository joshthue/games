Royal Draw Rush

Cards fall one at a time. Tap a column to drop the card in — it stacks on top, like Tetris.

Clear a row

When a row of five fills and makes a poker hand (a pair or better), it scores and clears, and everything above drops down. Bigger hands pay way more — a flush or a royal is a jackpot.

Don't choke the well

A junk row (no pair) doesn't clear — it sits there eating space. Let the stack cross the red line and it's game over. Aim your cards to build hands, not just fill holes.

It speeds up

The drop timer tightens every level. Clear two or more rows at once for a combo multiplier. Dawdle and the card auto-drops into the shortest column.
