Corners

Twenty-one pieces each. Every piece you play has to touch your own color corner to corner, never side by side. Box the others out; whoever has the fewest squares left when nobody can move wins.

How to play

The pieces

Everybody gets the same 21 pieces: every shape you can make from one to five squares. 89 squares in all.

Your first piece

It has to cover your starting square: your corner of the board, or the dot on your side in a one-on-one game.

Every piece after that

  • It must touch at least one of your own pieces corner to corner.
  • It may never touch your own pieces side to side.
  • Other colors don't matter. Touch them any way you like; that's how you block them.

Placing on a phone

  • Tap one of your pieces. It shows on the board where it fits.
  • Tap or drag on the board to move it. Rotate and Flip turn it around that spot.
  • A white outline means it fits; red means it doesn't. Tap Place.
  • The small dots are the squares you can grow from (turn them off in the Menu).

The end

When you can't fit any piece you're out; the others keep going. When everybody is out, every square you still hold is minus one. Play all 21 for +15, and +5 more if the single square went last. Highest score wins.
