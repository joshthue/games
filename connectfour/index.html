How to play

Take turns dropping discs into the columns. A disc falls to the lowest open slot.

First to line up four of your colour — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins. Fill the board with no four and it's a draw.

You're red. The bot is yellow. Pick your difficulty and whether you drop first in the menu.
