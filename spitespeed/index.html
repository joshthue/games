Spite Speed

Real-time Spite & Malice — no turns. You and the bot race at the same time to empty your goal pile onto the four shared centre stacks, built Ace → King.

Tap a card (your goal card, a hand card, or a side-pile top), then tap a centre to play it. Only the next rank fits; a ★ wild plays as anything. Fill a stack to the King and it clears away.

Stuck? Tap a hand card, then a side pile to park it — your hand refills to five. The centres are shared, so grab the rank you need before the bot does. First goal pile empty wins.
