6-Player Sit & Go

Blind speed

Turbo ≈ 20 minutes · Standard ≈ 30 · Marathon ≈ 40

Texas Hold’em

Everyone gets two private cards. Five community cards come out in the middle. Your hand is the best five cards you can make from all seven.

The betting

  • Preflop — two cards each, blinds are posted, betting starts left of the big blind.
  • Flop — three community cards, then betting.
  • Turn — a fourth card, then betting.
  • River — a fifth card, a last round, then showdown.

A raise has to be at least as big as the last one. Going all in for less than a full raise doesn’t reopen the betting for anyone who has already acted.

Hand rankings, best first

  • Straight flush · Four of a kind · Full house · Flush
  • Straight · Three of a kind · Two pair · Pair · High card

All in and side pots

If you go all in, you can only win the part of the pot you matched. Anything beyond it goes into a side pot the deeper stacks play for. Odd chips in a split pot go to the first player left of the button.

The tournament

Six players, 5,000 chips each. Blinds rise every few hands, so the table gets shorter and shorter. Bust and you’re out — last player holding all the chips wins.
