Shut the Box

Roll the dice, flip down tiles that add up to the roll. Keep going until you can't. Whatever's still standing is your score — shut them all and you've shut the box.

How to play

  • All the tiles start standing. Roll the two dice.
  • Flip down any standing tiles that add up to the roll: roll a 9 and you can shut the 9, or 1 + 8, 2 + 7, 3 + 6, 4 + 5, 1 + 2 + 6, 1 + 3 + 5, 2 + 3 + 4. Then roll again.
  • When a roll can't be made from what's standing, your turn is over. Your score is the total of the tiles still up. Low is good.
  • Flip every tile and you've shut the box: score 0, and you win on the spot.
  • One die: once every tile 7 and up is down, you can choose to roll just one die (if that rule is on).

Against the bots

Everybody plays their own box in turn. Lowest score wins. Normal bots play perfectly (the lowest average score possible). Easy bots grab the biggest tile they can.

On the phone

  • Tap tiles to pick them. When they add up to the roll, tap Shut.
  • Hint shows what perfect play would do: which tiles to shut, or whether to roll one die.
